How to grow a cube family

The goal: as many cubes as you can squeeze into your Cubby.

Make a cube

Pick a colour, 1 of 10 eyes, 1 of 10 mouths, doodle on its face and give it a name. You can make 10.

Roll it!

Every roll does something silly and earns sparkles ✨ and love 💖.

Hold hands

In the World, a cube with a full heart holds hands with another player's cube. Both players get a baby!

Watch them grow

Roll a baby 10 times and it grows up, ready to make babies of its own. Grandcubes!

Questions

Is it free?

Yes! Making cubes, rolling, the World and holding hands are all free. The shop only takes sparkles, which you earn by rolling.

Who is it for?

Silly people of all ages. A grown-up (16 or older) makes the account and little ones play along. You can try the cube on this page without an account.

Why only 3 letters in the chat?

So it stays friendly! You can say HI!, YAY or send a 💖, one message every 30 seconds, and messages vanish after a minute or two.

How do I get more than 10 cubes?

You can make 10 cubes by hand. After that, send cubes to the World and hold hands with other players' cubes: each time, a baby is born. Babies get one parent's eyes, one parent's mouth and a mixed-up colour.

What if someone draws something rude?

Tap their cube and press Report. A grown-up moderator can wipe the drawing, change the name or send the cube home.

Does it work on my phone or tablet?

Yes. Swipe on a cube or use the big arrow buttons. It runs in the browser, nothing to install.
